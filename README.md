I've always loved making things. I want to help build a world where everyone gets to.

Too many people spend their lives securing the basics: food, healthcare, a good education. I believe the next generation of AI, in software and in physical machines, can end those scarcities. I want to build that AI, and the human institutions that make an abundant world worth living in. This will take decades, so I'm starting with what I can do now: learning, building, and building teams.

In 10th grade I found there was almost nothing in rocketry for Indian school students, so I founded the Indian Students' Rocketry Association. We design, build and launch model rockets from scratch, backed by ₹1.3 lakh in institutional funding from Charutar Vidya Mandal.

I believe students working together create an impact adults can't. So with five friends I co-founded the Jeevartan Foundation. At first we did every task ourselves. It has now grown to 90 student volunteers who have served 7,450 meals, recycled 200 kg of plastic and vaccinated 31 stray dogs. In November 2026 I stepped down as president and handed Jeevartan to the juniors I mentored. That's what I'm proudest of: we built something that was bigger than any of us.

I also build AI. GOFI (Geniusly Organised Facilitating Intelligence) is my personal assistant; it runs entirely on my own machines and I improve it every day. TIDI (Terrestrial Interface for Dhairya's Intellgience) is my childhood RC car, which I rebuilt with a LiDAR head to be GOFI's body. There's also Stark, an autonomous humanoid robot I'm building with three teammates, where I'm learning reinforcement learning to train it.

Next, I'll study mathematics at university, the subject I've loved for as long as I can remember.

If you're working on AI, robotics or education, I'd love to talk.
